# a1_meet (0–12 s), visual-first cold open

Files: `shots/a1_meet.js` (it imports helpers from `shots/s1_meet_fx.js`; the s1 files are unchanged).
Final contact sheet: `out/preview/a1/final_sheet.jpg`.

- **0–4** Fade up from black, two sparks ignite (0.2 violet, 0.6 orange), vertebrae fly in and stack (Quill 0.4→2.8,
  Ember 0.8→3.2), the core grows through landed blocks, heads pop, eyes open (2.95 / 3.15). No captions. Name pins
  `Quill` / `Qwen3-4B` and `Ember` / `AFM-4.5B` show from 3.0 to 4.6, while the creatures stand still.
- **4–7** Prompt tiles appear (4.2) and enter both tails together (4.6–5.3; `The sky is` as square tiles, `Once upon a`
  as pills). The cores dim (wave armed at 5.0), then one `pose.wave` climbs both spines in lockstep (5.3→6.4, same value
  for both), with sparks flying from each block into the core. Both heads emit a token at 6.45 (`blue`, `time`).
- **7–10** Quill grins and lobs its own output token `blue` at Ember (throw 7.15). The hex glass field flashes up
  (7.62), the token shatters at **8.0** (lavender shards and dust, ring ripple, shake, flash), and Ember flinches. Then
  puzzled looks while the field dissolves.
- **10–12** Hold: `xray` 0→0.7 (9.9–10.9) with the glass blocks kept glowing in their own color, cores revealed, the 8
  port rings ignite bottom to top (10.15+) and settle at ~0.7. `neuralese` (136 px, teal, low center) appears at 10.35,
  `the language of hidden states` (40 px) at 10.7, and both fade from 11.82 to 12.0. Soft smiles.
- **End state** Home poses, xray 0.7, ports lit, link not grown, wave off, no inject.

Port opacity is held at ~0.7 rather than 1.2, because at xray 0.7 brighter rings bloom over the glass bodies and hide
them. Purity: re-rendering 8.3 after other times gives a max pixel difference of 0. Full-res frames take about 1.1–2.2 s.
